# Design decisions

<Lead>
  Every choice that shapes the architecture is written down once, when it is made: the context, the options that were
  considered, the decision and its consequences. A decision is never edited to say something else; a changed decision
  is a new record that names the one it replaces.
</Lead>

## Reading them

The records are short on purpose. The ones that explain the most about how delivery works are 0010, the schedule
living in Postgres; 0016, how claims stay fair and capped; and 0011, why the payload is stored as bytes. The ones that
explain the boundaries are 0002, the two planes; 0009, the contract and its mutual TLS; and 0005 with 0017, how events
travel between them.
